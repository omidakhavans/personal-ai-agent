---
title: Write LinkedIn
description: Adapt an approved reviewed article into a grounded professional post draft.
---

`write-linkedin` reads the approved blog and its matching review, then writes `linkedin-draft.md`. It does not inspect the repository, research resources, alter the canonical article, publish, or create X content.

The stage requires a matching review fingerprint and explicit approval. Its structured post paragraphs and main insight cite known evidence IDs before the runtime renders a `draft_for_review` artifact.

This is content transformation: a trusted canonical article is adapted to a new medium instead of independently reconstructing the engineering story.
